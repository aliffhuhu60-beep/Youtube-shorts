## Bahagian A — 5 skrip siap rakam (≈ 30–40 saat)

### #1 — The Bajau "Sea Nomads" Evolved Bigger Spleens  🧠 Body
**Title:** These People Evolved to Hold Their Breath Longer 🌊
```
HOOK:   Some humans have literally evolved to live underwater.
BODY:   The Bajau people of Southeast Asia have spent centuries free-diving for food,
        sometimes working underwater for hours a day.
        In 2018, scientists found their spleens are about fifty percent larger
        than their neighbours'.
        Why does that matter? Your spleen stores oxygen-rich red blood cells.
        When you dive, it squeezes — like an emergency oxygen tank.
        A bigger tank means longer dives. Natural selection, happening in real time.
LOOP:   So the next time you hold your breath… remember some humans have evolved to live underwater.
```
Visual: perahu lepa kertas · penyelam kertas · ilustrasi limpa sebagai "tangki oksigen" · peta Asia Tenggara.
Sumber: Ilardo et al., *Cell* (2018); University of Copenhagen press release.

### #2 — Saturn Would Float  🚀 Space
**Title:** Saturn Would Float in a Bathtub 🛁🪐
```
HOOK:   If you had a big enough bathtub, Saturn would float.
BODY:   Saturn is huge — about 95 times the mass of Earth.
        But it's made mostly of hydrogen and helium, the lightest elements there are.
        Its average density is lower than water.
        So in theory, drop Saturn into a giant ocean… and it would bob on the surface.
        The problem? You'd need a bathtub wider than Jupiter. And Saturn would leave a ring.
LOOP:   Which is exactly why, if you had a big enough bathtub…
```
Sumber: NASA Saturn Facts (science.nasa.gov/saturn).

### #3 — Rafflesia: The Flower That Smells Like Rotten Meat  🌊 Earth
**Title:** The World's Biggest Flower Smells Like a Corpse
```
HOOK:   The biggest flower on Earth has no leaves, no stem, no roots — and it stinks.
BODY:   Meet Rafflesia arnoldii, found in the rainforests of Sumatra and Borneo.
        It can grow up to one metre wide.
        It's a parasite — it lives inside a jungle vine and steals all its food.
        And it smells like rotting meat on purpose: to attract flies that carry its pollen.
        It blooms for only about a week. Then it collapses into black slime.
LOOP:   Beautiful, huge, and disgusting. The biggest flower on Earth.
```
Sumber: Britannica "Rafflesia"; Kew Gardens.

### #4 — The Lost Library That Burned Slowly  🏛️ History
**Title:** The Library of Alexandria Didn't Burn in One Day
```
HOOK:   Everything you were taught about the Library of Alexandria is probably wrong.
BODY:   The legend says one great fire destroyed all ancient knowledge in a single night.
        But historians think the real story is slower — and sadder.
        Julius Caesar's fire in 48 BC may have damaged part of it.
        After that, it was budget cuts, politics, and scholars being expelled
        that drained it over centuries.
        The greatest library in history didn't die in a fire. It died from neglect.
LOOP:   And that's why everything you were taught… is probably wrong.
```
Sumber: Britannica "Library of Alexandria"; World History Encyclopedia.

### #5 — What If the Moon Disappeared?  ❓ What If
**Title:** What If the Moon Vanished Tonight? 🌑
```
HOOK:   If the Moon vanished tonight, you'd notice the oceans first.
BODY:   Tides would shrink to about a third of their size — only the Sun would pull on them.
        Nights would be far darker. Animals that hunt or breed by moonlight would be confused.
        And over millions of years, Earth's tilt could wobble more,
        making climate swing wildly.
        The Moon isn't just decoration. It's Earth's stabiliser.
LOOP:   So keep an eye on the sky… because if the Moon vanished tonight…
```
Sumber: NASA "What if there were no Moon?"; Planetary Society.
