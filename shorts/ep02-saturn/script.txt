If you had a big enough bathtub, Saturn would float.
Saturn is huge. About 95 times the mass of Earth.
But it's made mostly of hydrogen and helium, the lightest elements there are.
So its average density is lower than water.
Drop Saturn into a giant ocean, and it would bob on the surface.
The problem? You'd need a bathtub wider than Jupiter.
And Saturn would leave a ring.
Which is exactly why, if you had a big enough bathtub...
